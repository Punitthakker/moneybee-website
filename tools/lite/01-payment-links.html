payment links

Send a link. Get paid.

A payment link is a short web address that opens a secure payment page. You type the amount, send the link to your customer, and they pay from their phone. You do not need a website, a card machine or a developer.

how it works

How it works

  1. Open the MoneyBee Lite app and enter the amount and what it is for.
  2. Share the link on WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS or email.
  3. Your customer taps the link and pays.
  4. You see the payment in the app straight away.

who uses them

Who uses payment links

  • Cafes and home kitchens taking orders over WhatsApp.
  • Salons, clinics and studios taking a deposit to hold a booking.
  • Shops selling through Instagram.
  • Freelancers and agencies collecting for a project or a retainer.

What you can do with a link

  • Take a deposit now and the balance later.
  • Bill the same customer every week or every month.
  • Split one order into several payments.
  • See who has paid and who has not, in one list.

questions

Questions people ask

Do I need a trade licence?
Yes. You apply with your trade licence and Emirates ID, from your phone.
Do my customers need an app?
No. The link opens in their browser.
Can my staff send links?
Yes. Give them the cashier role. They can take payments without seeing your finances.
Is it secure?
Card details are held in a token vault and never stored on your phone. PCI DSS v4.0 certification is under way.
