on WhatsApp

Get paid in the chat where you already sell.

Most small businesses in the UAE already take orders on WhatsApp. The slow part is the payment: sharing bank details, waiting for a transfer, asking for a screenshot. A payment link removes all three steps.

step by step

Step by step

  1. Agree the order with your customer in WhatsApp as usual.
  2. Open MoneyBee Lite, type the amount and a short note such as "12 coffees, Tuesday".
  3. Tap share and choose WhatsApp. The link drops into the chat.
  4. Your customer taps it, pays, and comes back to the chat.
  5. You get a notification. No screenshot needed.

three ways

Three ways to use it

Before delivery
Send the link with the order confirmation, cook or pack once it is paid.
For bookings
Send a deposit link to hold the slot.
For regulars
Set a weekly or monthly link so you never have to ask.

Tips that get you paid faster

  • Put the item and the date in the note so the customer recognises it.
  • Send the link in the same message as the order summary.
  • If a link is still unpaid, you can see it in the app and send one reminder.
