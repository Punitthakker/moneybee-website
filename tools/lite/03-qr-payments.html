how it works

How it works

  1. Create your QR code in the MoneyBee Lite app.
  2. Print it and place it at the counter, on the table or on the menu.
  3. The customer scans, enters or confirms the amount, and pays.
  4. You can see the payment in the app.

where it works

Where it works best

  • The lunch rush, when one card machine means a queue.
  • Market stalls, kiosks and pop-ups with no fixed till.
  • Tables, so guests pay without waiting for the bill.
  • Delivery riders collecting at the door.
