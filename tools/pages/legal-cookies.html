Cookie policy

No tracking. No ads. Just three small settings that stay on your device.

Last updated 3 November 2026

moneybee.world, operated by Neon Global FZ-LLC (RAKEZ, UAE), does not set advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies. The site uses your browser's local storage, a cookie-like technology, only for the essential items below. They never leave your device.

NameWhat it doesKept
mb-themeRemembers light or dark themeUntil you clear it
mb-hiveRemembers the honey you earned exploring the site and the high score in the gameUntil you clear it
mb-cookie-okRemembers that you closed this noticeUntil you clear it

Third-party content

Links to partner sites, LinkedIn, X and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own cookie policies. We do not embed their trackers on our pages.

Your choices

You can clear these items at any time in your browser settings under site data. The site still works without them; it will just forget your theme and game progress.

Changes and contact

If we ever add non-essential cookies we will update this page and ask for your consent first. Questions: pt@moneybee.world.
