Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Neon Global FZ-LLC, registered in the Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ), United Arab Emirates ("MoneyBee", "we"), handles personal data when you visit moneybee.world or contact us. We follow the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and other laws that apply to us.

Personal data processed when a business uses MoneyBee products (for example, payment or onboarding data) is covered by the product agreement and its data processing terms, not by this website policy.

1. What we collect

  • When you contact us by email or WhatsApp: your name, contact details, company and what you tell us.
  • When you browse: our host keeps standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time, for security and to keep the site running.
  • On your device: a few essential settings in your browser's local storage (see our cookie policy). These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this site and we do not sell personal data.

2. Why we use it

  • To answer your questions and discuss MoneyBee products with you (our legitimate interest, or steps before a contract at your request).
  • To keep the site secure and working (legitimate interest).
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run the site and our business, under contract and only for that purpose:

ProviderPurpose
NetlifyWebsite hosting and delivery
ZohoBusiness email
WhatsApp (Meta)Messages you choose to send us

We may also share data with authorities when the law requires it, or with a buyer of our business, subject to this policy.

4. International transfers

Some providers process data outside the UAE. Where that happens we rely on safeguards permitted by UAE law, such as contractual protections.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to deal with them and for a reasonable period after, unless the law requires longer. Host logs are kept for a short period set by our host.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy. Write to pt@moneybee.world. We will reply within the time the law sets. You may also complain to the UAE Data Office.

7. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with modern security headers. Access to enquiries is limited to people who need it.

8. Children

The site is for businesses and is not aimed at children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
