MoneyBee app privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy covers the MoneyBee Lite app for Android and iPhone (package world.moneybee.lite) and the merchant services it connects to. It is provided by Neon Global FZ-LLC, registered in the Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ), United Arab Emirates ("MoneyBee", "we"). The app is for businesses that take payments with MoneyBee and the people they add to their team. Our website has its own privacy policy.

1. What we collect

  • Sign-in: your mobile number and the one-time code we send to it; if you set one, your email address and a password (we store only a salted hash of the password).
  • Business verification: business details, trade licence, owner name, Emirates ID number and image, and other documents you upload or photograph in the app; if you choose UAE PASS, the identity details UAE PASS shares with your consent.
  • Your business profile: logo, contact details and refund policy you add; these are shown to your customers on your payment page.
  • Payments: payment links you create, the amounts, references and any customer name or WhatsApp number you add, and payment records such as amount, card brand, the last four digits of the card and the customer's country. Card numbers are entered on Worldpay's or Botim's own payment fields, never in the app.
  • Security: the IP address, approximate country and device type of each sign-in, so we can warn you about sign-ins from a new country.
  • Notifications: a push token for your device, if you allow notifications.
  • Crash reports: when the app fails, the error message, technical trace, platform and app version, so we can fix it.
  • Face ID or fingerprint: checked by your phone only; we never receive biometric data.

The app has no advertising or analytics trackers. We do not track you across other apps or websites and we do not sell personal data. The app does not use your microphone or location.

2. Why we use it

  • To provide the service you signed up for: sign-in, payment links, payments, refunds, settlement and reports (contract).
  • To verify businesses and prevent fraud, money laundering and terrorist financing, as payment rules require (legal obligation).
  • To keep accounts secure and the service working (legitimate interest).
  • To send payment alerts you asked for (consent, which you can withdraw in your phone settings).

5. How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account is open. Payment, refund, settlement and verification records are kept for [the period required by UAE payment and anti-money-laundering rules: to confirm] after the account closes. Crash reports and security logs are kept for [period: to confirm].

6. Deleting your account

In the app, open Hive, Account, Delete account. An owner can close the business account: team members lose access, sign-in details and push tokens are deleted, open payment links stop working, and the account cannot be used again. A team member can remove their own access. Records we must keep by law (section 5) are kept and then deleted. If a dispute or refund is still open, the app tells you, and you can delete the account once it is settled. You can also email pt@moneybee.world to ask us to delete your account.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive a copy. Write to pt@moneybee.world.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and MoneyBee is encrypted (HTTPS). Your session is stored in your phone's secure keychain or keystore. Access to merchant data is limited to people who need it.

9. Children

The app is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.
